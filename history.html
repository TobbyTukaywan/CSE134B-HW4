History & Legacy

History

Tak and the Power of Juju

Tak and the Power of Juju was originally concepted and pitched by Avalanch Software to THQ and Nickelodeon in 1998, eventually being completed and releasing in October 15, 2003. It released to moderate success, selling over a million units, and made history as Nickelodeon's first video game not based off of an existing property.

Tak 2: The Staff of Dreams

Released less than a year later, on October 11, 2004, Tak 2: The Staff of Dreams was met with greater critical success than the first, receiving better reviews, but less commercial success, selling not quite a million units. However, it did have a collaboration with Burger King.

Tak: The Great Juju Challenge

Tak: The Great Juju Challenge had a challenged development. Partway through development, Avalanch Software was bought by Disney. However, they decided to let Avalanch complete work on Tak 3 before ending their relationship with Nickelodeon for good. The game reuses many assets from previous games, likely due to time constraints, but still ended up as a complete game that received favorable reviews at the time.

Legacy

After the Trilogy

A couple years after the release of the final game in the trilogy, on August 31, 2007, Nickelodeon released the first episode of the Tak and the Power of Juju animated TV series, making history as their first 3D animated series produced fully in-house. It did not have much critical acclaim and deviated heavily from the source material. The show spawned two spinoff games, Tak and the Guardians of Gross and Tak: Mojo Mistake, then ended on January 24, 2009.

The Fan Community

Over 20 years later, there is still a small but dedicated fanbase for these old games. The fans are mostly spread over small communities, but some relatively big-name YouTubers have covered Tak as well, bringing new attention to this old franchise.

The Future of Tak

On March 21, 2018, THQNordic announced their plan to bring back many old THQ titles to newer systems, either as remakes/remasters or even with brand new games. On that list of old games was Tak and the Power of Juju. However, it has been over 8 years since then with no mention of a new Tak game, so the future of the series remains uncertain.
