About

About the site

This site was made as part of the class CSE-134B at UCSD, taught by professor Thomas Powell. It was also made because I adore the Tak series and wanted to share my knowledge and love of it. It is not currently a comprehensive compendium of all knowledge about Tak, but hopefully it serves as an informational site for people wanting to learn what it's all about.

About me

My name is Abdurrahman Syed, and I was born on April 3, 2002 (559 days before the release of Tak and the Power of Juju). Tak and The Power of Juju was the first game I ever laid my eyes on, and I've been in love with the series ever since. I am currently pursuing a bachelors in Computer Science and hoping to make a career in game development.
