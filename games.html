Games

The Tak series consists of three mainline games released between 2003 and 2005 which were developed by Avalanch Software and published by THQ and Nickelodeon. There were also several handheld versions and games based off of the cartoon, but these are not covered here and frankly not very good.

Tak and the Power of Juju

Released: October 15, 2003

The first game in the trilogy. A boy named Tak must save his village from the evil shaman Tlaloc with the help of powerful Juju spirits in this collectathon platformer.

Tak 2: The Staff of Dreams

Released: October 11, 2004

The sequel. Tak is back, wielding new Juju powers, and this time he must enter the world of dreams to rescue a mysterious princess from the Dream Guardian in this linear action platformer.

Tak: The Great Juju Challenge

Released: September 19, 2005

The grand finale. Tak and his old pal Lok compete together in the Great Juju Challenge for the fate of their village in this co-op time-attack adventure.
